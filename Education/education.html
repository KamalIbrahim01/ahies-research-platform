Research Question 1

School Attendance During the Previous Three Months

Key Finding

School attendance remained relatively stable between 2022 and 2024, with approximately 47–49% of respondents reporting attendance during the previous three months.

Research Question 2

Highest Educational Attainment

Key Finding

Primary education remained the most common highest level of educational attainment across the study period, while only about 6% of respondents had attained tertiary education.

Research Question 3

Reasons for Never Attending School

Analysis available for 2023 and 2024.

Key Finding

Family restrictions were the leading reported barrier to school attendance in both 2023 and 2024. Financial difficulties and perceptions that education was not valuable were also important barriers.

Research Question 4

Who Finances Education?

Key Finding

Fathers remained the largest single source of education financing. However, the proportion financed jointly by both parents increased from 19.05% in 2022 to 27.56% in 2024.
