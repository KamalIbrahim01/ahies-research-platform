Research Question 1

Reported Illness by Sex

Comparison of recent illness among male and female respondents across the AHIES survey period.

Key Finding

Female respondents consistently represented a larger share of reported illness cases than male respondents across the survey period.

Research Question 2

Healthcare Consultation

Examining healthcare consultation among respondents reporting illness during the survey period.

Key Finding

Just over half of respondents reporting illness consulted a health practitioner, with the consultation rate declining slightly across the study period.

Research Question 3

Current NHIS Coverage

Examining current National Health Insurance Scheme coverage among respondents across the AHIES survey period.

Key Finding

Current NHIS coverage remained relatively stable across the survey period. This indicator measures current coverage rather than insurance retention.
