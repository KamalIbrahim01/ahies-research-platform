ABOUT THE PROJECT

About the AHIES Research Platform

A research-oriented web platform developed to explore education, health and labour indicators from Ghana's Annual Household Income and Expenditure Survey (AHIES) through interactive analytics, visualizations and modern data technologies.

THE PLATFORM

About the Project

The AHIES Research Platform was developed as an interactive analytical environment for exploring selected socioeconomic indicators contained within Ghana's Annual Household Income and Expenditure Survey (AHIES).

Rather than presenting raw survey tables, the platform transforms complex datasets into accessible research outputs through interactive charts, statistical summaries and analytical dashboards. It demonstrates how modern data engineering, database management and business intelligence technologies can be combined to support evidence-based research and decision-making.

OBJECTIVES

Project Objectives

The platform was designed to bridge the gap between large-scale household survey data and practical research by providing an intuitive interface for exploring key socioeconomic indicators.

  • Provide an accessible platform for exploring AHIES education, health and labour data.
  • Support evidence-based policymaking through interactive analytical outputs.
  • Demonstrate modern data engineering, database management and web development practices.
  • Encourage transparent, data-driven research using official national survey data.

METHODOLOGY

Data Preparation and Analysis

The 2022, 2023 and 2024 AHIES datasets were processed using Python. Relevant variables were extracted, cleaned and standardized before being integrated into a unified analytical dataset suitable for longitudinal analysis.

The processed dataset was loaded into a MySQL database hosted on Microsoft Azure. SQL queries power the analytical results presented throughout the platform, while a Flask REST API provides secure communication between the database and the web application.

Treatment of Missing Values

Missing observations were intentionally preserved throughout the data preparation process. Many AHIES variables are conditionally administered, meaning that unanswered questions often indicate that a question was not applicable rather than representing a numerical zero.

Preserving missing values maintains the integrity of the original survey and prevents misleading analytical conclusions that could arise from inappropriate imputation.
